About

Team Zhallenged was founded in 2025 by a group of Danish players tired of solo queue and ready to compete as a unit. We play in the amateur Danish circuit and have been steadily climbing since our first match.

We're not the flashiest team in the bracket. We grind the fundamentals, adapt in-series, and take every game as a new challenge — hence the name. If you want to scrim or have questions, reach out.
